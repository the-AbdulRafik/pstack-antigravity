# NOTICE

## pstack-antigravity Lineage & Attributions

This project, **pstack-antigravity**, is an open-source port and native adaptation of Lauren Tan (@poteto)'s SpaceX-inspired engineering playbooks and agent workflows, engineered specifically for the **Google Antigravity** agentic workspace.

### Upstream Sources

1. **Original Creator & Concept:**
   - **Lauren Tan (@poteto)**: Created the original `pstack` for the Cursor IDE, introducing the 24 engineering principles, the operational playbooks, and the anti-slop subagent architecture.
   - Upstream repository: [cursor/plugins (plugins/pstack)](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/plugins/pstack)
   - Copyright (c) 2025-2026 Lauren Tan (@poteto). Licensed under the MIT License.

2. **Cross-Harness Research & Adaptations:**
   - **Michael Denyer**: Authored `pstack-claude`, providing foundational cross-runtime research and adaptations for Claude Code, Codex, and other AI coding harnesses.
   - Upstream repository: [michael-denyer/pstack-claude](https://github.com/michael-denyer/pstack-claude)
   - Copyright (c) 2026 Michael Denyer. Licensed under the MIT License.

3. **Google Antigravity Native Adaptation:**
   - Authored by the `pstack-antigravity` contributors.
   - Adapts the principles and playbooks to Google Antigravity's progressive disclosure skills format, isolated git workspaces (`Workspace: "branch" | "share"`), first-class interactive plan artifacts (`RequestFeedback: true`), and Gemini Flash reasoning effort tiers.
